What I'm building right now

See the money your store is losing.

Octivis is the AI perception layer for physical retail. It connects the POS, accounting software, supplier records, and payment data stores already use to find the losses draining their margins. Stores only pay from what Octivis recovers.

01 / Supplier recovery

Find supplier overcharges hiding in the books.

Octivis compares the records stores already keep so owners can catch invoice discrepancies and recover lost margin.

Books + invoices → recoverable loss
02 / Payment intelligence

Surface payment gaps before they become routine.

POS and transaction signals reveal card anomalies, missed payments, and register exceptions that would otherwise stay buried.

POS + payments → clear exceptions
03 / Fuel reconciliation

Catch fuel billing errors before they drain margin.

Octivis checks fuel loads against rack benchmarks, contract terms, billed gallons, taxes, and freight to surface discrepancies worth recovering.

Fuel records + invoices → recoverable gaps
